Release notes

What changed in every version of Poni, newest first. Poni updates itself, so installed copies get each of these automatically.

  1. 0.6.0Latest

    November 13, 2026

    • Poni stays free, with no time limit. Everything you use today stays free: every built-in tab including Agents, the Shelf with AirDrop and quick actions, system activities and replacing the macOS volume and brightness pop-ups, the notch on every screen, hiding it for chosen apps, and the command bar. Poni never interrupts you to sell anything: Pro features carry a quiet "Pro" tag, and Settings → License opens only when you click it.
    • Poni Pro, if you'd like more (one-time purchase, up to 3 Macs):
      • Smart Shelf: right-click an image in the Shelf to Copy text from image (the text is copied and also lands in the Shelf) or Remove background (the subject, on a transparent PNG). Both run on your Mac with macOS's own Vision.
      • Tab profiles: save the tabs in your notch as a profile, e.g. Work or Home, under Settings → Notch → Tab profiles. Switch from the menu bar or the command bar (type the profile's name), or let a profile switch itself on at set hours, on weekdays or every day.
      • Your own accent colour for buttons, rings and highlights in the notch, under Settings → Notch → Look.
      • Plus the Liquid Glass and Pill themes and unlimited Gallery plugins, as before.
    • License page: shows what's always free first, then what Pro adds.

  2. 0.5.13

    November 13, 2026

    • Out of the way in full screen: under Settings → Notch → In full-screen apps, keep showing the notch, show only short notices (volume, brightness, battery…) while it stays closed and out of sight, or hide it completely. It works per display, for full-screen apps, Split View, games and slideshows.
    • Hide for these apps: add apps under Settings → Notch; while one of them is in front, the notch stays closed and only shows notices. Your shortcut still opens it any time.
    • Sound output: a short notice when sound moves to other speakers, a display, AirPlay or headphones. Turn it off under Settings → Activities → Sound output.
    • Charger power: plugging in now shows the charger's wattage too, e.g. "76% · 96 W".
    • Agents (new tab, turn it on in the Gallery): see what Claude Code and Codex are doing, which session is waiting for you, and which just finished, with a nudge beside the notch. Open brings that terminal forward. For Claude Code, Poni asks before adding a few hooks to its settings (a backup is kept, and Disconnect removes only Poni's). Everything stays on your Mac.
    • Lighter on your battery: the notch does much less work while music or videos play, and closed Settings and Gallery windows no longer keep working in the background.
    • Fixed: in Music, the times beside the progress bar no longer wrap onto two lines for videos of 10 minutes or longer, and videos over an hour show hours.
    • Opening Poni again from Finder or Spotlight while it runs now opens Settings.
    • For plugin authors: notch.ai.agents(), notch.ai.connectAgents(), notch.ai.disconnectAgents() and notch.ai.openAgent(id) (permission ai-usage).

  3. 0.5.12

    November 13, 2026

    • Scrolling no longer changes the tab: scrolling over the open notch could switch to another tab without a click. Tabs now change only when you click them or use the keyboard.
    • Swiping between tabs is now optional: turn on Settings → Notch → Swipe sideways to switch tabs if you want it. It's off by default, and it's stricter: only a deliberate sideways swipe counts, and a vertical scroll never turns into one.

  4. 0.5.11

    November 13, 2026

    • The notch on every screen: pick Every screen under Settings → Notch → Show the notch on. The notch you can open moves to whichever display your pointer is on, and the other displays show the closed notch with the same notices beside it.
    • Seek, shuffle and repeat in Music: drag or click the progress bar to jump within the song. Shuffle and repeat buttons appear for players that share them, such as Music and Spotify.
    • A month calendar in Today: the new calendar button shows the whole month, with a dot on days that have events. Click a day to see its events.
    • Quick actions in the Shelf: the new Actions button combines images into one PDF, makes images smaller for chats and email, or zips everything. Right-click an item to convert it to JPEG or PNG. Your original files are never changed.
    • Battery time left: after you unplug, the notch shows how long the battery will last once macOS has an estimate.
    • Bluetooth devices: turn on Settings → Activities → Bluetooth devices to see a keyboard, mouse or speaker connect and disconnect. macOS asks for Bluetooth access when you turn it on.
    • Swipe between tabs: with the notch open, swipe sideways with two fingers on the trackpad (or Shift-scroll with a mouse) to move to the next or previous tab.
    • Animation speed: choose Relaxed, Normal or Brisk under Settings → Notch.
    • For plugin authors: notch.media.seek(seconds), notch.media.control('shuffle' | 'repeat'), a draggable ui.progress(value, { action }), and notch.calendar.events(days, from) for any range up to 42 days.

  5. 0.5.10

    November 13, 2026

    • Liquid Glass: the Glass theme is now Liquid Glass and uses macOS's own Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later. Older macOS versions keep the blurred look.
    • New theme, Liquid Glass Pill: Pill's card floating below the notch, with a Liquid Glass surface. Pick it in Settings → Notch. Like the other extra themes it comes with Poni Pro.

  6. 0.5.9

    November 13, 2026

    • Poni Pro is now a one-time purchase: pay $15 once on Gumroad and keep Pro forever, with no subscription. One license works on up to 3 Macs. Open Settings → License to buy or to paste your key.

  7. 0.5.8

    November 13, 2026

    • Prayer Times no longer stalls at prayer time: the adhan sound now loads in the background, so the plugin no longer stops with "render() ran too long" when a prayer begins, even with a long audio file or Bluetooth speakers.

  8. 0.5.7

    November 13, 2026

    • Permissions only when you ask: onboarding has a new page that explains each permission (Music, Calendar, Reminders, Downloads, Screenshots), and the macOS prompt appears only after you press Allow. Today and Music show an Allow button instead of prompting on their own.
    • Now Playing from any app: Poni now reads the media playing in any app, not just Music and Spotify. Browsers, podcasts and video players show up in the notch and can be paused or skipped.
    • Brightness on external displays: the brightness HUD follows the display under the pointer, and works with monitors controlled by BetterDisplay, MonitorControl or Lunar.
    • A smaller meeting wing: the wing shows a short countdown ("12m") before a meeting and a clear Join while the call can be joined. Meetings that have ended no longer stay in the wing, and the Today tab says when the Join button will appear.
    • Smoother themes: switching to Pill or Glass now morphs the notch instead of jumping.

  9. 0.5.6

    November 12, 2026

    • Secure randomness and hashes for plugins: the new notch.crypto gives plugins secure random bytes, UUIDs, and SHA-256 / SHA-1 / MD5 hashes, so the Dev Toolbox plugin can make strong passwords.

  10. 0.5.5

    November 12, 2026

    • Plugins can open folders: notch.open("~/Downloads") and other file paths now open in Finder, so the Quick Links plugin can keep your favourite folders one click away.

  11. 0.5.4

    November 12, 2026

    • A new Plugin Gallery: the same night look as Settings, with a search field, category filters, colourful cards and clear Add / Install buttons. Tabs that are already in your notch are marked in green.
    • The menu bar panel closes when you click elsewhere: clicking another app, the desktop or switching apps now closes it, like a normal menu.

  12. 0.5.3

    November 12, 2026

    • Drag to reorder now works: drag a tab in the menu bar panel's "In your notch" row, or a row in Settings → Notch → Tab order, and the other tabs make way as you go. A click on a tab in the panel still opens its settings.

  13. 0.5.2

    November 12, 2026

    • The new Poni look everywhere: the fringe-notch logo now shows in the menu bar panel, Settings and the introduction, and the mascot says hello on the last step of the introduction (it blinks, and smiles when you point at it).

  14. 0.5.1

    November 12, 2026

    • Drag to reorder tabs: drag the tab icons in the menu bar panel, or the rows under Settings → Notch → Tab order, to change the order of the notch's tabs. The rows make way as you drag.
    • The tab row in the menu bar panel now fits however many tabs you have.

  15. 0.5.0

    November 12, 2026

    • A new look for Settings: a sidebar of coloured icons and pages that explain themselves at a glance: General, Notch (with a live preview and picture-based theme choice), Activities, Keyboard (your shortcuts drawn as keys), Shelf, License and every tab.
    • A new menu bar menu: Poni's icon now opens a panel with what's in your notch and big buttons for the command bar, the Shelf, sending from your phone and the Gallery.
    • A new introduction: five short steps in the style of our website, each with a moving picture of the feature: the notch opening, tabs flipping, notices beside the notch, and the command bar answering as you type.

  16. 0.4.4

    November 12, 2026

    • Choose what's in your notch: new installs start with four tabs, Music, Today, Weather and Timer. Focus, Prayer Times and Mirror now wait in the Gallery under Built into Poni, one click from being added; anything can be taken off the notch there too. If you already use Poni, your tabs stay as they are.

  17. 0.4.3

    November 12, 2026

    • GitHub Actions (new in the Gallery): your workflow runs and latest deployment for the repositories you list, with the running build counting beside the notch and a green or red notice when it finishes. Add a fine-grained token with read access to Actions for private repositories.
    • For plugin authors: notch.fetch(url, { method, headers, body }, cb); callbacks now also get the HTTP status code, and notch.features tells which of these a Poni version supports.

  18. 0.4.2

    November 12, 2026

    • Music from your browser: the Music tab now also shows YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify Web, SoundCloud and other players in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge and Brave, with the title, artwork and progress, and play/pause, next and previous. Poni only looks at a browser while it's playing sound. For the buttons and progress, turn on Allow JavaScript from Apple Events once: in Safari under Develop, in Chrome-based browsers under View → Developer. Without it you still see the tab's title.

  19. 0.4.1

    November 12, 2026

    • Screenshots in the Shelf: every new screenshot or screen recording lands in the Shelf, with a thumbnail beside the notch for a moment, ready to drag into a chat or an email. Poni reads the folder your screenshots are saved to (the Desktop unless you changed it), so macOS asks once for access to it. Turn it off in Settings → General → System activities.

  20. 0.4.0

    November 12, 2026

    • Command bar: press ⌥Space anywhere and type into the notch. Do sums ("12*1.5", "200*15%"), convert units ("5 km to mi", "30 c to f") and currencies ("100 usd to idr"), open apps and files, start a timer ("timer 5"), add a reminder ("remind call the bank 3pm"), open the Shelf, or search the web. ↑ ↓ choose, Enter runs (results are copied), Esc closes. Change or turn off the shortcut in Settings → General.

  21. 0.3.10

    November 12, 2026

    • Cursor in AI Usage: the AI Usage plugin (from the Gallery, version 1.2.0) can now show this month's Cursor usage next to Claude Code and Codex: what you've spent of your plan, or premium requests on older plans, and when it resets. It's off until you turn on Cursor monthly usage in the plugin's settings. It uses the Cursor login already on your Mac to ask cursor.com, the same way Cursor's own Settings → Usage does; the login goes nowhere else.

  22. 0.3.9

    November 12, 2026

    • Send files from your phone: in the Shelf, click From phone and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. Pick photos or files on the page that opens and they land straight in the Shelf. It works over your Wi-Fi only, with a one-time link, and stops when you click Done (or after 10 quiet minutes). The first time, macOS may ask whether Poni can accept incoming connections: choose Allow.

  23. 0.3.8

    November 12, 2026

    • Timer: a new built-in tab with 1, 3, 5, 10 and 15-minute presets, or type your own length ("25" or "1:30"). The countdown shows beside the notch, and when time's up Poni plays a sound and sends a notification (both can be changed in the plugin's settings). A stopwatch is one tap away. Both keep counting while your Mac sleeps or Poni restarts.

  24. 0.3.7

    November 12, 2026

    • Add reminders from the notch: type in the field under Reminders in the Today tab and press Enter. A time at the end sets when it's due ("Call the bank 3pm", "Standup 9:30"); without one it's due today. It lands in your default Reminders list.
    • For plugin authors: notch.reminders.add(title, due), and clear: true on ui.input empties the field after Enter.

  25. 0.3.6

    November 12, 2026

    • Reminders in Today: reminders due today, and overdue ones in red, now sit under your agenda in the Today tab. Click the circle to mark one done; it's saved in the Reminders app. macOS asks once for access to Reminders. Turn it off in the Today plugin's settings.
    • For plugin authors: notch.reminders.list(), .complete(id) and .status() with the new reminders permission, and style: 'plain' for icon-only buttons such as checkboxes.

  26. 0.3.5

    November 12, 2026

    • Camera and microphone dots: while any app uses the camera, a green dot shows beside the notch; for the microphone, an orange one. Handy in calls, and they sit next to whatever else the notch shows. Turn them off in Settings → General → System activities.

  27. 0.3.4

    November 12, 2026

    • Quick Look in the Shelf: click an item, then press Space to preview it right inside the notch, as in Finder: images, PDFs, text, videos and documents. ← and → move between items, Esc or Space goes back. You can also right-click an item and choose Quick Look. The notch stays open while you look.

  28. 0.3.3

    November 12, 2026

    • Downloads beside the notch: downloads from Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Arc and Edge show a progress ring with the percentage (or the size so far when the browser doesn't say how big the file is). When a download finishes you see a green check with the file name, and the file is waiting in the Shelf. macOS asks once for access to the Downloads folder. Both can be turned off in Settings → General → System activities.

  29. 0.3.2

    November 11, 2026

    • Mirror: a new built-in tab that shows your camera in the notch, so you can check how you look before a call. The image is mirrored like a real mirror; Flip shows you as others see you. The camera is on only while the tab is open and turns off as soon as the notch closes. macOS asks for camera access the first time.
    • For plugin authors: ui.camera({ height, radius, mirrored }) with the new camera permission.

  30. 0.3.1

    November 11, 2026

    • Update alerts in the notch: when a new version of Poni is out, it shows beside the notch and in Notification Center, and the open notch gets a blue Update button. Click either to see what's new and install it. You'll see this from the next update after this one.
    • Smoother shadow: the open notch's shadow no longer ends in a straight line at its sides.

  31. 0.3.0

    November 11, 2026

    • Poni speaks English now. English is the default; switch to Bahasa Indonesia in Settings → General → Language, or on the first page of the introduction. The change applies right away, including in plugins. If you used Poni before this version, it stays in Indonesian.
    • Plugins follow the app's language: the built-in and gallery plugins come in both languages. Plugin authors can use notch.locale and a locales block in manifest.json (see the SDK docs).

  32. 0.2.1

    November 11, 2026

    • Gantikan pop-up volume dan kecerahan macOS: nyalakan di Pengaturan → Umum → Aktivitas sistem. Tombol volume, senyap dan kecerahan lalu diatur Poni, levelnya tampil di samping notch, dan pop-up besar macOS tidak muncul lagi. Butuh izin Aksesibilitas sekali. Tahan ⇧⌥ untuk langkah yang lebih halus, seperti di macOS.
    • Ikon headphone: saat suara keluar lewat AirPods atau headphone, HUD volume menampilkan ikon headphone. Matahari meredup saat kecerahan rendah.

  33. 0.2.0

    November 11, 2026

    • NotchKit sekarang bernama Poni. Situs barunya akan ada di useponi.app.
    • Semua milik Anda ikut pindah: pengaturan, urutan tab, Shelf, plugin dari galeri dan kunci lisensi dibawa otomatis saat Poni pertama dibuka. Aplikasi di folder Applications juga berganti nama menjadi Poni.app, lalu terbuka lagi sendiri.
    • Izin perlu diberikan ulang: karena identitas aplikasinya baru, macOS akan meminta lagi izin Kalender, Automation dan Notifikasi. Jika Anda memakai "Buka saat login", nyalakan lagi di Pengaturan → Umum. macOS mungkin juga bertanya sekali apakah Poni boleh membaca kunci lisensi lama dari Keychain; pilih Always Allow.

  34. 0.1.4

    November 11, 2026

    • Baterai AirPods: saat AirPods atau headphone Bluetooth tersambung, baterainya tampil sebentar di samping notch, dan muncul lagi dengan warna merah muda saat tinggal 20 %. Tanpa izin Bluetooth. Bisa dimatikan di Pengaturan → Umum → Aktivitas sistem.

  35. 0.1.3

    November 11, 2026

    • Pengingat azan: saat masuk waktu sholat, Jadwal Sholat menampilkan pop-up di notch, mengirim notifikasi, dan membunyikan suara pilihan Anda: suara macOS atau file azan Anda sendiri. Bisa tanpa suara saat Subuh. Tombol Hentikan tersedia selama suara berbunyi. Atur di Pengaturan → Jadwal Sholat.
    • Statistik fokus: tab Focus menampilkan grafik menit fokus 7 hari terakhir dan total minggu ini.
    • Perbaikan: ikon plugin kembali tampil di jendela Pengaturan dan Galeri.

  36. 0.1.2

    November 11, 2026

    • Gabung rapat dari notch: tab Hari Ini menemukan tautan Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Jitsi dan Whereby di acara kalender, lalu menampilkan tombol Gabung mulai 10 menit sebelum rapat. Pengingat dan sayap notch memakai ikon video untuk rapat.
    • Password tetap aman: salinan dari pengelola password (1Password, Bitwarden, KeePassXC, aplikasi Passwords) tidak pernah diberikan ke plugin mana pun.
    • Plugin baru di galeri: Clipboard. Riwayat teks yang Anda salin, salin lagi dengan satu klik, sematkan yang sering dipakai. Pasang lewat tombol + di notch.

  37. 0.1.1

    November 10, 2026

    • Tampilan baru yang sama dengan halaman NotchKit: huruf Onest dan JetBrains Mono, ikon Lucide, dan tata letak baru untuk Musik, Focus (cincin hitung mundur), Hari Ini, Jadwal Sholat (grid lima waktu), Cuaca dan Shelf.
    • Gerak baru: notch membuka dengan pantulan halus, halaman bergeser saat pindah tab, teks dan lirik naik saat berganti, matahari cuaca berputar, titik acara yang sedang berlangsung berdenyut.
    • Volume, kecerahan dan baterai tampil sebentar di samping notch saat notch tertutup (bisa dimatikan di Pengaturan → Umum).
    • Pintasan ⌃⌥⌘N membuka notch dari aplikasi mana pun; ← → atau 1–9 untuk pindah tab, Esc untuk menutup. Pintasan bisa diganti.
    • Musik: artwork untuk Apple Music, dan baris Latin di bawah lirik Jepang, Korea dan Mandarin.
    • Untuk pembuat plugin: konsol di Pengaturan, ikon Lucide (lucide:<nama>), komponen baru (ui.ring, sorotan yang meluncur, daftar yang masuk bergantian). Panduan: https://muhghazaliakbar.github.io/notchkit-releases/sdk.html
