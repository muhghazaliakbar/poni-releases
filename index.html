Your MacBook's notch can do more than hide the camera. 

Poni puts your music, a focus timer, today's calendar, the weather and prayer times right below the camera. Point at the notch and everything opens. Add plugins from the gallery, or write your own in JavaScript.

System activity

When the notch is closed, its wings keep working

Volume, display brightness and battery appear briefly on either side of the notch. Drag the sliders or tap the buttons below, and the small notch responds just like it does on your Mac.

Poni
With coffee and the same old song

Keyboard shortcut, from any app

Tap the keys, or press ⌃⌥⌘N on your keyboard. While it's open, ← → or 1–9 switch tabs and Esc closes it.

Volume follows the active output device and switches automatically when your AirPods connect. The standard macOS pop-ups still appear, and you can turn off each activity separately in Settings.

Plugin SDK

Write your own plugins in the JavaScript you already know

A plugin is one folder with a manifest.json and a main.js. Plugins don't draw their own UI: they return a declarative layout that SwiftUI renders natively, so everything feels like part of macOS.

1var clicks = 0;2 3// What the notch shows when it's open.4function render() {5  return ui.vstack([6    ui.text('Hello!', { style: 'title' }),7    ui.button('Tap me', 'click', { symbol: 'hand.tap.fill' }),8    ui.text(clicks + ' taps', { color: 'secondary' })9  ]);10}11 12// Shown next to the notch when it's closed.13function compact() {14  return clicks > 0 ? { symbol: 'sparkles', text: clicks + ' taps' } : null;15}16 17function onAction(action) {18  if (action === 'click') clicks++;19}

Install

Installing the beta

The beta isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS holds it back the first time you open it. You only need to confirm once; later updates install on their own.

The full guide, including how to verify the app's signature, is in INSTALL.md.

  1. 1

    Download the DMG, then drag Poni to Applications.

  2. 2

    Open Poni.

    macOS says it won't open Poni. Click Done.

  3. 3

    Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.

    Scroll down, click Open Anyway, then enter your Mac password. On macOS 14 Sonoma, you can instead Control-click Poni in Applications and choose Open.
